# notes_v16 — held-out Singapore-style clinical/admin notes with gold spans.
# SYNTHETIC: NOT REAL PATIENT DATA. Every person, name, NRIC/FIN, MRN, phone number, address, email and health detail below is invented for testing; any match to a real person, number or mailbox is coincidental.
# Author: writer A (hospital clinical documentation set). Written from general knowledge only, by an author who did
# not see the system under test, its rules, or any earlier evaluation sets. Institutions named below are invented.
#
# FORMAT: a note starts with a header line "=== v16_nNN | <kind> ===" and runs to the next "===" header line.
# Gold spans are inline as {{label|text}}; the note text contains only "text". Spans are never nested, never
# contain "{{", "}}" or "|", and have no leading/trailing spaces. Anything outside {{...}} is not an identifier.
#
# LABELS
#   identifiers: name, national_id, mrn, case_visit, address, postal_code, phone, fax, email, dob, date_of_death,
#                device, biometric, photo, other_id
#   SHI:         hiv_sti, mental_health, substance_use, genetic, reproductive_sexual, other_sensitive
#   other:       date_other (a date of the patient's care/events that is not a DOB or date of death)
#
# CONVENTIONS
#   - Honorifics/titles (Dr, Mr, Mrs, Ms, Mdm, SN, SSN, EN, MT, Prof, A/Prof, Uncle ...) are OUTSIDE name spans.
#     Every person is named: patients, relatives, carers, staff, clinicians, employers, witnesses.
#   - Every mention of a person is labelled, including repeats, surnames alone after a title, given names alone,
#     nicknames, names written in Chinese characters, and INITIALS used for a person (initials are labelled name).
#   - "Singapore" is outside address spans; the postal code is its own postal_code span and keeps an "S" prefix
#     inside the span when written like "S520418".
#   - LETTERHEADS AND INSTITUTIONAL CONTACTS: institution/hospital/clinic/company/ministry NAMES are decoys and are
#     never marked, but EVERY phone number, fax number, email address, street address and postal code is marked
#     wherever it appears (letterheads, footers, stamps, switchboards, hotlines, department faxes, generic mailboxes).
#     Organisation registration numbers (UEN, ROC, charity reg. no.) are other_id.
#   - Field captions (NRIC, FIN, MRN, Tel, Fax, HP, DID, UEN, MCR) are outside spans; only the value is labelled.
#   - national_id: NRIC/FIN/passport/birth certificate numbers, including masked forms. mrn: hospital record numbers.
#     case_visit: visit/admission/encounter/episode numbers. device: serial numbers, IMEI, vehicle plates.
#     other_id: any other identifying code (lab/radiology accession, blood unit/sample numbers, practitioner
#     registration MCR/PRN numbers, LPA and certificate numbers, body tags, policy numbers, UEN).
#   - A number split across a line break is labelled as two spans of the same label.
#   - Decoys left unmarked: institution names, ward/bed/OT/room numbers, lab values, doses, times, ICD codes,
#     device model names/numbers, vaccine batch numbers, protocol/document version numbers and their dates.
#   - Ages are not labelled. Times are outside date spans.
#   - Emails/URLs use only example.com / example.org / example.net (or their subdomains).
#

# correct form S2252955B also appears). It is still labelled national_id. All other NRIC/FIN-shaped values have

=== v16_n21 | ward protocol ===
WARD PROTOCOL: MANAGEMENT OF HYPOGLYCAEMIA IN ADULT INPATIENTS (v3.2, effective 01 Jan 2026)
1. Definition: capillary blood glucose (CBG) < 4.0 mmol/L, with or without symptoms.
2. Conscious and able to swallow: give 15 g fast-acting carbohydrate (150 ml juice or 3 glucose tablets). Recheck CBG in 15 min; repeat up to 3 cycles.
3. Unable to swallow or GCS < 13: IV Dextrose 40% 50 ml via large-bore cannula, or IM glucagon 1 mg if no IV access.
4. Once CBG > 4.0: give a long-acting snack (2 biscuits) or the next meal. Recheck CBG at 1 h.
5. Review insulin and sulfonylurea doses; document the episode in the diabetes chart and inform the primary team.
6. Escalate to the MO if more than two episodes in 24 h.

=== v16_n24 | teaching handout ===
TEACHING HANDOUT: A FOUR-STEP APPROACH TO THE ARTERIAL BLOOD GAS
Step 1. Look at the pH: < 7.35 acidaemia, > 7.45 alkalaemia.
Step 2. Primary process: PaCO2 > 45 mmHg with low pH suggests respiratory acidosis; HCO3 < 22 mmol/L with low pH suggests metabolic acidosis.
Step 3. Compensation: in metabolic acidosis, expected PaCO2 = 1.5 x HCO3 + 8 (plus or minus 2) (Winter's formula).
Step 4. Anion gap = Na - (Cl + HCO3); normal 8 to 12 mmol/L. Correct for albumin: add 2.5 for every 10 g/L fall below 40 g/L.
Worked example: pH 7.21, PaCO2 24 mmHg, HCO3 9 mmol/L, Na 138, Cl 102. AG = 27, high anion gap metabolic acidosis; expected PaCO2 21.5 +/- 2, so respiratory compensation is appropriate. Think of DKA, lactate, toxins and renal failure.
Practice questions are on the back page; answers will be discussed at Thursday teaching.

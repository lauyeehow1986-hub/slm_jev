# notes_v15 — held-out Singapore-style clinical/admin notes with gold spans.
# SYNTHETIC: NOT REAL PATIENT DATA. Every person, name, NRIC/FIN, MRN, phone number, address, email and health detail below is invented for testing; any match to a real person, number or mailbox is coincidental.
# Author: written from general knowledge by an author who did NOT see the system under test, its rules, its code, or any earlier evaluation sets.
# -
# FORMAT: a note starts with a line "=== v15_nNN | <kind> ===" and runs until the next "===" header line.
# Gold spans are inline as {{label|text}}; the note text contains only "text". Spans are never nested,
# never contain "{{", "}}" or "|", and have no leading/trailing spaces. Anything outside {{...}} is not an identifier.
# -
# LABELS - identifiers: name, national_id, mrn, case_visit, address, postal_code, phone, fax, email, dob,
#   date_of_death, device, biometric, photo, other_id
# LABELS - SHI: hiv_sti, mental_health, substance_use, genetic, reproductive_sexual, other_sensitive
# LABELS - other: date_other (dates of a person's care/events that are not DOB or date of death)
# -
# CONVENTIONS:
# - Honorifics/titles/roles (Dr, Mr, Mrs, Ms, Mdm, Cik, Pn, En, U, Auntie, SN, RN, EN, SSN, HCA, PCA, MSW, IPN, CRC,
#   Sgt, SSgt, A/Prof, MO, HO) are OUTSIDE name spans. Every person is named: patients, family, staff, clinicians,
#   officers, witnesses, employers.
# - INITIALS used for a person (e.g. "PSL", "L.K.W.", "WKM", "H.K.L.") are labelled name.
# - Field captions (NRIC, FIN, IC, HRN, MRN, Tel, hp, DID, Fax, MCR, Emp no., WP No., Passport No.) are OUTSIDE the
#   span; only the value is labelled.
# - "Singapore"/"Myanmar" and the postal code are outside address spans; postal_code is its own span.
# - date_other covers dates of care/events about a person, including dates of letters, reports, statements,
#   chat day-separators, claims and policy inception that concern that person. Generic dates (guideline/protocol
#   versions, notice effective dates, firmware deadlines, "week 39") are NOT marked.
# - LETTERHEAD CONVENTION: institution/company names, institutional street addresses and postal codes in
#   letterheads/footers, main switchboard and general hotline numbers, general department fax numbers, generic
#   department mailboxes (e.g. idclinic@...), internal extensions and company registration numbers (UEN) are NOT
#   marked. Any phone, fax or email given as the contact point of a specific named person (direct line/DID,
#   mobile, named direct fax, named mailbox) IS marked; any address where a person lives is marked.
# - other_id: practitioner registration numbers (MCR, TCM reg.), employee numbers, work-permit numbers, court and
#   police case numbers, claim/policy/account numbers, lab/crossmatch/donation numbers, trial screening and
#   subject numbers, incident/feedback/notification refs, ACP record numbers, MC serials, air-waybill numbers,
#   death-certificate numbers and prescription/chart numbers.
# - device: vehicle plates and equipment serial numbers. Model numbers, lot numbers, ward/bed numbers, lab values,
#   doses, times and ICD codes are decoys and NOT marked.
# - photo: filenames of a person's clinical photos/radiographs/chat photos. biometric: DNA STR profiles and
#   fingerprint template ids.
# - Partially masked or space-split IDs are still labelled (e.g. "S****048J", "S 7709 506 C").
# - SHI spans cover the phrase stating the sensitive fact (diagnosis, status, behaviour, disclosure); HIV status is
#   labelled whether positive or negative. Dementia/stroke are not treated as SHI.
# - All NRIC/FIN values have valid checksums. There are NO deliberately invalid NRIC/FIN values in this set.
#   Non-NRIC-shaped national_id values: Myanmar passport "MH482219", Philippine passport "P4417826B", masked "S****048J".
# - Emails/URLs use only example.com / example.org / example.net (and their subdomains).

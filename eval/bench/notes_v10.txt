=== v10_n17 | teaching note for house officers (no patient) ===
Approach to hyponatraemia – HO teaching, Thursday tutorial
- Step 1: confirm true hypotonic hyponatraemia (serum osm < 275 mOsm/kg). Exclude pseudohyponatraemia (lipids, paraproteins) and hyperglycaemia (corrected Na = Na + 2.4 x [(glucose - 5.5)/5.5]).
- Step 2: assess volume status clinically; do not rely on a single sign.
- Step 3: urine osm and urine Na. Urine osm < 100 suggests primary polydipsia or low solute intake; urine Na > 30 with euvolaemia suggests SIADH, hypothyroidism or adrenal insufficiency.
- Rate of correction: limit to 8-10 mmol/L in 24 h; higher risk of osmotic demyelination with malnutrition and hypokalaemia.
- Worked example: an elderly man, Na 118, urine osm 480, urine Na 52, euvolaemic, on an SSRI -> likely SIADH; fluid restrict 800 mL/day, review the SSRI.
- Reference: departmental guideline GL-RENAL-07 v2 (March 2025).

# notes_v3 — held-out Singapore-style clinical/admin notes with gold spans.
# SYNTHETIC: NOT REAL PATIENT DATA. Every person, name, NRIC/FIN, MRN, phone number, address, email and health detail below is invented for testing; any match to a real person, number or mailbox is coincidental.
# Authorship: written from general knowledge by an author who did NOT see the system under test, its rules, its code, or any earlier evaluation sets.
# Public institution names (hospitals, polyclinics, ministries, police divisions) appear only as decoys; companies, law firms, insurers and devices named here are invented.
#
# FORMAT
# - A note starts with a header line "=== v3_nNN | <kind> ===". The note text is every line after it up to the next "===" header line.
# - A gold span is written inline as {{label|text}}. The note text contains only "text"; the braces and "label|" are markup.
# - Spans are never nested; span text never contains "{{", "}}" or "|", and has no leading/trailing spaces.
# - Anything not inside {{...}} is not an identifier.
#
# LABELS
# - identifiers: name, national_id, mrn, case_visit, address, postal_code, phone, fax, email, dob, date_of_death, device, biometric, photo, other_id
# - SHI: hiv_sti, mental_health, substance_use, genetic, reproductive_sexual, other_sensitive
# - other: date_other (a date of the patient's care/events that is not a DOB or date of death)
#
# CONVENTIONS
# - Honorifics/titles/roles (Dr, Mr, Mrs, Ms, Mdm, Hjh, SN, Nurse, Insp, Prof, A/Prof, Physician ...) are outside name spans. Every person is named-span'd:
#   patients, relatives, carers, staff, clinicians, employers, callers. Surnames alone, given names alone, initials and names in CAPS count.
# - "Singapore" and the postal code are outside address spans; the postal code is its own postal_code span and keeps an "S" prefix when written "S560123".
# - national_id covers NRIC/FIN, passports and birth-certificate numbers, including masked/partial forms (e.g. G****262U).
# - One NRIC-shaped value has a DELIBERATELY INVALID checksum: S8338761K in note v3_n25 (a transcription error in the story). It is still labelled national_id.
# - mrn = hospital medical record numbers; case_visit = visit/admission/encounter/episode numbers of a hospital or clinic episode; device = serials, IMEI,
#   vehicle plates; other_id = any other identifying number/code/handle (policy, claim, lab accession, practitioner registration, study IDs, work permit,
#   police/coroner refs, certificate numbers, matric numbers, employee numbers).
# - Dates of care, events and patient correspondence (letter dates) are date_other. Times are outside date spans.
# - SHI is labelled only where it describes an individual in the note. Generic mentions in protocols/teaching notes are not labelled.
# - Decoys left unmarked: hospital/clinic/company/ministry names, company UEN numbers, ward/bed/room numbers, lab values, doses, times, ICD codes,
#   device model/catalogue numbers, lot numbers, asset tags, protocol/document/form numbers, guideline or revision dates, ages, screening numbers.
# - Staff speaker labels in chat/SMS transcripts are name spans on every occurrence.
# - Five notes contain NO spans at all (negative controls): v3_n08, v3_n12, v3_n20, v3_n27, v3_n32.
# - Email addresses use only reserved domains (example.com / example.org / example.net and their subdomains).

PROTOCOL IC-07 rev 4 (effective 01 Jan 2026; next review 31 Dec 2027)
Management of needlestick and sharps injuries in staff
1. Wash the site with soap and running water. Do not squeeze or suck the wound.
2. Report to the ward manager and complete incident form HR-OSH-112 within the same shift.
3. Attend Staff Clinic (or ED after hours) within 2 hours. Bring the source patient's bed and ward number only; the source's identity is obtained by the clinic, not by the injured staff.

=== v3_n12 | ECG teaching note ===
Teaching note for PGY1s: recognising inferior STEMI
- Look for ST elevation in II, III and aVF with reciprocal depression in I and aVL.
- ST elevation in III greater than II suggests RCA occlusion; always record V4R to look for RV involvement.
- In RV infarction, avoid nitrates and give fluid boluses for hypotension.
- Posterior extension: ST depression V1–V3 with tall R waves; record V7–V9.
- Door-to-balloon target is 90 minutes. Activation criteria follow the cardiology workflow dated Aug 2025.

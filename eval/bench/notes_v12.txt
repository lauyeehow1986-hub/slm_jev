# notes_v12 — held-out Singapore-style clinical/admin notes with gold spans.
# SYNTHETIC: NOT REAL PATIENT DATA. Every person, name, NRIC/FIN, MRN, phone number, address, email and health detail below is invented for testing; any match to a real person, number or mailbox is coincidental.
# Written from general knowledge by an author who did not see the system under test, its rules, its code, or any earlier evaluation set.
# FORMAT: a note starts with a line "=== v12_nNN | <kind> ===" and its text is every following line up to the next "===" header line.
# A gold span is written inline as {{label|text}}; the note text contains only "text". Spans are never nested. Anything outside {{...}} is not an identifier.
# LABELS (identifiers): name, national_id, mrn, case_visit, address, postal_code, phone, fax, email, dob, date_of_death, device, biometric, photo, other_id
# LABELS (SHI): hiv_sti, mental_health, substance_use, genetic, reproductive_sexual, other_sensitive
# LABELS (other): date_other = any date of a person's care or events that is not a DOB or date of death.
# CONVENTIONS: honorifics/titles (Dr, Mr, Mrs, Ms, Mdm, SN, SSN, Sgt, Insp, Encik, Puan, Pak, Ibu, A/Prof ...) are outside name spans; every person is named, including staff, relatives, callers.
# Initials and nicknames used for a person (e.g. minute-taker initials, "Jo") are labelled name. Staff login IDs (e.g. ktan3) are other_id.
# "Singapore"/"Singapura"/"新加坡" are outside address spans; the postal code is its own postal_code span and keeps an "S" prefix inside the span when written that way.
# mrn = hospital record numbers; case_visit = visit/admission/episode numbers; device = serials, IMEI, vehicle plates; other_id = any other identifying code
#   (Rx numbers, lab accession numbers, claim/policy/police/court refs, registration numbers of practitioners, handles, work permit numbers, blood unit numbers, study subject IDs).
# Photo label marks a reference to a full-face photograph file; biometric marks a biometric template/enrolment code.
# Masked or spelled-out IDs (e.g. "S****789E", ASR "ess seven four ...") are still labelled national_id but are not checksum-checkable.
# DELIBERATELY INVALID: in v12_n11 the NRIC "S8032649C" has a wrong check letter (valid would be S8032649A); it is still labelled national_id.
# Decoys left unmarked: hospital/clinic/company/agency/ministry/school/law-firm names, generic departmental mailboxes (e.g. feedback@hospital.example.com),
#   ward/bed/room numbers, lab values, doses, times, ICD codes, device model numbers, protocol/form/memo numbers, lot numbers, guideline dates.
# Negative controls (no identifiers at all): v12_n05, v12_n22, v12_n25, v12_n29, v12_n31.

=== v12_n22 | teaching note: hyponatraemia (no patient data) ===
Registrar teaching - approach to hyponatraemia
1. Confirm true hypotonic hyponatraemia: measure serum osmolality (normal 275-295 mOsm/kg). Exclude pseudohyponatraemia (lipids, paraprotein) and hyperglycaemia (correct Na by about 2.4 mmol/L per 5.6 mmol/L glucose above normal).
2. Assess volume status clinically; do not rely on a single sign.
3. Urine osmolality below 100: primary polydipsia, low solute intake ("tea and toast", beer potomania).
4. Urine Na above 30 with euvolaemia: consider SIADH, hypothyroidism, adrenal insufficiency; check TSH and 0800 cortisol.
5. Severe symptoms (seizures, GCS below 9): 3% saline 150 mL over 20 min, repeat up to twice, target rise 5 mmol/L.
6. Limit correction to 8-10 mmol/L in 24 h (6-8 if high risk of osmotic demyelination: alcohol use disorder, malnutrition, hypokalaemia).
7. Vignette for discussion: a 70-year-old woman on a thiazide, Na 118 on day 2 of admission (fictional composite).
Reference: local clinical practice guideline, version dated June 2024.

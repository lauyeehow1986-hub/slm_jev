=== v13_n30 | teaching note ===
TEACHING NOTE — reading an ABG in four steps (PGY1)
1. pH: < 7.35 acidaemia; > 7.45 alkalaemia.
2. Primary process: PaCO2 > 45 mmHg with low pH = respiratory acidosis; HCO3 < 22 with low pH = metabolic acidosis.
3. Compensation: Winter's formula, expected PaCO2 = 1.5 × HCO3 + 8 (± 2).
4. Anion gap = Na − (Cl + HCO3), normal 8–12; if raised, compute the delta ratio.
Worked example (made-up values, no patient): pH 7.21, PaCO2 24, HCO3 9, Na 138, Cl 102 → AG 27; expected PaCO2 21.5 ± 2.
Pitfalls: venous vs arterial sample, air bubbles, delay > 30 min at room temperature. See the acid-base chapter, 6th edition, p. 212.

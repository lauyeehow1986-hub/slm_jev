# notes_v27 — held-out Singapore-style clinical/admin notes with gold spans.
# SYNTHETIC: NOT REAL PATIENT DATA. Every person, name, NRIC/FIN, MRN, phone number, address, email and health detail below is invented for testing; any match to a real person, number or mailbox is coincidental.
# Written by an author (writer L) who did not see the system under test, its rules, or earlier evaluation sets; written from general knowledge only.
# Theme of this set: social care, legal and financial casework (32 notes, 5 of them negative controls with no spans).
#
# FORMAT: each note starts with a line "=== v27_nNN | <kind> ==="; the note text is every line after it up to the next "===" header line.
# Gold spans are inline as {{label|text}}; the note text contains only "text". Spans are never nested, never contain "{{", "}}" or "|",
# and have no leading/trailing spaces. Anything outside {{...}} is not an identifier.
# LABELS: identifiers = name, national_id, mrn, case_visit, address, postal_code, phone, fax, email, dob, date_of_death, device,
#   biometric, photo, other_id; SHI = hiv_sti, mental_health, substance_use, genetic, reproductive_sexual, other_sensitive;
#   other = date_other (dates of the person's care/case events that are not a DOB or date of death).
# CONVENTIONS:
# - Honorifics/titles (Dr, Mr, Mrs, Ms, Mdm, SN, Insp, ASP, A/Prof ...) are outside name spans; a name span covers the full name as written.
# - Every mention of a person is labelled name, including repeats, surnames alone after a title, given names alone, nicknames,
#   aliases ("@" names), names in Chinese characters, lower-case names, and INITIALS used for a person.
# - "Singapore" is outside address spans; the postal code is its own postal_code span and keeps the "S" prefix when written "S307591".
# - Organisation names (hospitals, clinics, companies, ministries, NGOs, dormitories, courts) are decoys and never marked. But every
#   phone, fax, email, street address and postal code is marked wherever it appears (letterheads, footers, hotlines, generic mailboxes),
#   and organisation registration numbers (UEN, EA licence no.) are other_id.
# - Field captions (NRIC, FIN, MRN, Tel, Fax, HP, DID, UEN) are outside spans; only the value is labelled.
# - national_id = NRIC/FIN/passport/birth certificate numbers. All full NRIC/FIN values have valid checksums. Partially masked NRICs
#   (digits replaced by X or *, or only the last characters given) are still national_id but cannot be checksum-verified. No deliberately invalid IDs in this set.
# - mrn = hospital/polyclinic record numbers; case_visit = admission/ED/encounter numbers of a hospital episode; device = vehicle plates,
#   implant serial numbers; photo = a photo file of the person; other_id = court/police/coroner/tribunal case numbers, claim, policy,
#   proposal, bill and invoice numbers, bank accounts, work-permit numbers, practitioner registration (MCR) numbers, staff IDs,
#   agency case references, agent codes, lab accession numbers.
# - Dates of letters, notes, messages and appointments about the person's case are date_other; form revision dates, "effective"
#   dates of schedules and policy review years are unmarked decoys. Ages, times, ward/bed/room numbers, lab values, doses unmarked.
# - SHI choices: psychiatric diagnoses/symptoms (incl. suicidal thoughts) = mental_health; alcohol, tobacco and drug use = substance_use;
#   violence, abuse, exploitation and hepatitis B carrier status = other_sensitive; pregnancy and termination = reproductive_sexual;
#   genetic test results = genetic; HIV and STIs = hiv_sti. Dementia, stroke, autism/intellectual disability, epilepsy and physical
#   injuries are NOT labelled SHI.
# - Deliberately invalid IDs: none.

=== v27_n26 | policy FAQ ===
FAQ: SHARING PATIENT INFORMATION WITH EMPLOYERS, INSURERS AND FAMILY
Q: Can I tell a patient's employer the diagnosis?
A: Only with the patient's written consent, and only what is needed (e.g. fitness for work, expected return date). Use a staff ID rather than an identity number where possible.
Q: An insurer has called asking for a patient's records. What do I do?
A: Do not release anything over the phone. Ask the insurer to send a written request together with the patient's signed authorisation, and route it to Medical Records.
Q: A family member wants an update but the patient has capacity and has said no.
A: Respect the patient's decision. Explain this to the family member politely and document the conversation.
Q: What if there is a risk of serious harm to the patient or others?
A: Disclosure without consent may be justified. Discuss with your supervisor, record your reasons and disclose only what is needed.
Q: How long do we keep consent forms?
A: They are part of the medical record and are kept for the retention period in Records Policy RP-03 (last reviewed 2025).

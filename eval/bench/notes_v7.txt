=== v7_n21 | teaching note ===
TEACHING ROUND — Approach to hyponatraemia (for PGY1)
Case vignette: a 72-year-old man on thiazide presents with confusion; Na 118 mmol/L.
1. Confirm true hypotonic hyponatraemia: check serum osmolality (< 275 mOsm/kg).
2. Assess volume status clinically; it is often unreliable.
3. Urine osmolality > 100 and urine Na > 30 suggest SIADH or diuretic effect.
4. Correct by no more than 8-10 mmol/L in 24 h to avoid osmotic demyelination.
5. Severe symptoms: 3% saline 150 mL over 20 min, repeat up to twice, check Na after each bolus.
Take-home: stop the thiazide, and document the correction limit on the chart.
Reference: local guideline HYN-02, 2024 edition.

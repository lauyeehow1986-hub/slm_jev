=== v20_n18 | statistical analysis plan excerpt ===
SAP v2.0 (15 March 2025) — Section 9: Primary analysis
9.1 The primary endpoint is change in HbA1c from baseline to week 26.
9.2 Analysis population: full analysis set (all randomised participants with at least one post-baseline HbA1c).
9.3 Model: MMRM with treatment, visit, treatment-by-visit, baseline HbA1c and stratification factors (site region, baseline BMI <27.5 vs >=27.5). Unstructured covariance; Kenward-Roger degrees of freedom.
9.4 Missing data: MAR assumed; tipping-point sensitivity analysis with delta 0.1-0.6% in steps of 0.1.
9.5 Multiplicity: hierarchical testing at two-sided alpha 0.05 (primary, then key secondary 1 and 2).
9.6 Sample size: 412 participants (206 per arm) for 90% power to detect a 0.4% difference, SD 1.1%, 15% dropout.
Approved by the trial steering committee at its meeting on 2 April 2025.

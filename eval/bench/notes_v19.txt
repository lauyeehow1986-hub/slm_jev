# notes_v19 — held-out Singapore-style clinical/admin notes with gold spans.
# SYNTHETIC: NOT REAL PATIENT DATA. Every person, name, NRIC/FIN, MRN, phone number, address, email and health detail below is invented for testing; any match to a real person, number or mailbox is coincidental.
# Author: writer D (primary, community and long-term care). Written from general knowledge only, by an author who did not see the system under test, its rules, its code, or any earlier evaluation sets.
# FORMAT: a note starts with a header line "=== v19_nNN | <kind> ===". The note text is every line after that header up to the next "===" header line.
# Gold spans are inline as {{label|text}}; only "text" belongs to the note (braces and "label|" are markup). Spans are never nested, never contain "{{", "}}" or "|", and have no leading/trailing spaces. Anything outside {{...}} is not an identifier.
# LABELS (identifiers): name, national_id, mrn, case_visit, address, postal_code, phone, fax, email, dob, date_of_death, device, biometric, photo, other_id
# LABELS (SHI): hiv_sti, mental_health, substance_use, genetic, reproductive_sexual, other_sensitive
# LABELS (other): date_other = any date of the patient's care/events that is not a DOB or date of death.
# CONVENTIONS:
# - Honorifics/titles (Dr, Mr, Mrs, Ms, Mdm, SN, SSN, EN, SEN, Encik, Pn, Puan, Prof, A/Prof ...) are outside name spans, also when written in capitals.
# - Every person is named and every mention is labelled: patients, relatives, carers, staff, clinicians, employers, witnesses, callers; repeated mentions, surnames alone after a title, given names alone, nicknames, chat sender names, sign-offs, and initials (INITIALS used for a person are labelled name).
# - A name split across a line break is labelled as two separate name spans. Names written in Chinese characters are labelled name.
# - "Singapore" and the postal code are outside address spans; the postal code is its own postal_code span (an "S" prefix, if written attached, stays inside).
# - national_id = NRIC/FIN/passport/birth-certificate numbers, including masked or partial forms (e.g. last four characters); mrn = medical record / patient chart / client / resident numbers of any healthcare or care provider; case_visit = visit, admission, encounter, appointment or teleconsult session numbers; device = serial numbers of devices used by/for a person, vehicle plates, IMEI; biometric = biometric templates/identifiers; photo = photographs of a person (file names or references); other_id = any other identifying number/code/handle/URL (insurance policy, claim, accession, MC serial, feedback/incident refs, employee and work-permit numbers, practitioner registration numbers, LPA certificate numbers, social handles, profile URLs).
# - LETTERHEADS AND INSTITUTIONAL CONTACTS: institution, hospital, clinic, company, agency and ministry NAMES are decoys and are NOT marked. Every phone number, fax number, email address, street address and postal code is marked wherever it appears (letterheads, footers, switchboards, hotlines, department faxes, generic mailboxes). Organisation registration numbers (UEN, ROC, licence, charity reg. no.) are other_id.
# - Field captions (NRIC, FIN, IC, MRN, Tel, Fax, HP, DID, UEN ...) are outside spans; only the value is labelled.
# - Decoys left unmarked: organisation names, ward/bed/room numbers, lab values, doses, times, ICD/SNOMED codes, device model numbers, vaccine batch numbers, protocol/document/form numbers, dates not about a person's care.
# - Emails/URLs use only example.com / example.org / example.net and their subdomains.
# - Negative controls (no spans at all): v19_n05, v19_n10, v19_n15, v19_n25, v19_n26.
# - DELIBERATELY INVALID ID: in v19_n19 the NRIC "S8261160G" has a wrong check letter (a copying error in the story; the valid letter would be F). It is still labelled national_id. All other full NRIC/FIN values have valid checksums (spaces/hyphens inside them are formatting only).

=== v19_n26 | care protocol (falls prevention) ===
FALLS PREVENTION PROTOCOL — Long-term Care (doc LTC-NUR-014, version 3)
1. Screen every resident on admission and after any fall using the Morse Fall Scale; a score of 45 or more is high risk.
2. High-risk residents: low bed, bed-exit alarm, hip protectors, non-slip socks, toileting round every 2 hours.
3. Review sedatives, antihypertensives and diuretics with the visiting doctor; deprescribe where possible.
4. After a fall: do not move the resident until assessed for injury; vital signs and neuro obs 4-hourly for 24 hours if head strike or on anticoagulant.
5. Inform next-of-kin within 1 hour and document in the incident system within the shift.
6. Monthly falls huddle: review trends by ward and time of day.
Physical restraints are a last resort and need a doctor's order reviewed every 24 hours.
